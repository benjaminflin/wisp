Trigger

A quick tap of the hold key latches recording on; tap again to stop. Set the window to 0 to disable latching. The hold key needs Accessibility access.

Transcription

A short clip is skipped when almost no 20 ms frame rises above this level, and any transcript Whisper is not confident about is discarded. Room tone is steady and speech is bursty, so quiet talkers still get through. Raise it only in a noisy room.

Cleanup with Qwen

—

Qwen fixes punctuation and filler words and applies spoken self-corrections such as “scratch that” or “actually, make it…”. If Ollama is unreachable the raw transcript is used.

Audio & output

Auto-paste sends ⌘V to whatever app is focused, so the words land where you are typing. It needs Accessibility access; without it the transcript still reaches your clipboard.
